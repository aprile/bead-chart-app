    Calibration

    Real beadwork usually comes out a little bigger than the bead specs, because of thread and tension. Measure a piece you've made and the app will use your numbers for that bead size and stitch.

    • Count at least 10 beads or rows: the more, the more accurate.
    • Width: measure along one row, from the edge of the first bead to the edge of the last. In brick and two-drop brick, don't include the half-bead step of the next row.
    • Height: measure a number of rows from top to bottom. In flat peyote, measure the beads in one column, without the half-bead step of the next column.
    • You can fill in just one of the two. The other keeps using the spec.

    Saved charts keep their bead counts; their finished size is worked out again with your measurements.
